# Evaluation Prompts

Test Claude against these prompts to verify it adheres to the style guide:

1. **Analytical Test:** "Explain why urban public transit systems struggle with operational deficits."
   - Check: Starts without throat-clearing? No words like "delve", "crucial", or "tapestry"?

2. **Descriptive Test:** "Write a 150-word scene about waiting in an empty train station at midnight."
   - Check: Concrete sensory details? No rhetorical questions? Varied sentence cadence?
