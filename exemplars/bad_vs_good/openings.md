# Exemplars: Openings & Tone

## Case 1: Analytical Draft
- **Bad (Standard AI):** 
  "The rapid evolution of modern technology has ignited a vibrant debate among thinkers worldwide, serving as a testament to humanity's relentless pursuit of innovation."
- **Good:** 
  "Within eighteen months, machine learning shifted from an academic curiosity into basic infrastructure."
- **Why:** Removes empty filler (*vibrant debate, testament to*) and anchors the point immediately in concrete observations.

## Case 2: Personal Narrative
- **Bad (Standard AI):** 
  "Have you ever wondered what it feels like to confront your deepest fears? For me, this transformative journey began on a crisp autumn morning."
- **Good:** 
  "The gym floor was freezing through my socks, and I had ten minutes left before the doors locked."
- **Why:** Cuts the generic hook question and places the reader directly into physical sensory details.
