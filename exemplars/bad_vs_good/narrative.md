# Exemplars: Narrative & Reflective Writing

## Case 1: Overcoming Obstacles
- **Bad (Standard AI):** 
  "Navigating this unforeseen setback was a journey marked by profound personal growth, ultimately fostering a sense of resilience and broadening my horizons."
- **Good:** 
  "The motor failed on the third run. Instead of ordering a replacement and waiting two weeks, I dismantled the casing, checked the wiring with a multimeter, and found the cracked solder joint."
- **Why:** Replaces abstract virtue-signaling (*resilience, profound growth, navigating*) with the physical problem and concrete solution.

## Case 2: Intellectual Curiosity
- **Bad (Standard AI):** 
  "I have always harbored a deep and enduring passion for the intricate wonders of computer science and mathematical discovery."
- **Good:** 
  "I spent three days trying to optimize a knapsack algorithm until I realized my bottleneck was memory allocation, not the recurrence relation."
- **Why:** Replaces empty claims of "passion" with evidence of obsession and specific problem-solving.
