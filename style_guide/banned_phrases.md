# Banned Tropes and Vocabulary

## Words to Eliminate
- **Verbs:** delve, foster, harness, leverage, underscore, navigate, encompass, beacon
- **Nouns:** tapestry, realm, testament, paradigm, symphony, journey
- **Adjectives/Adverbs:** pivotal, crucial, paramount, intricate, vibrant, deeply, immensely

## Habitual Formats to Eliminate
- Starting follow-up paragraphs with *"Moreover,"*, *"Furthermore,"*, or *"It is worth noting that..."*
- "Both sides" cliches: *"a double-edged sword"*, *"at the crossroads"*, *"two sides of the same coin"*
- Starting essays with rhetorical questions like *"Have you ever wondered..."*
